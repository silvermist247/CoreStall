Privacy

This page explains what personal information this site handles, and why. The site is run by Seth Barton (CoreStall).

1. What the site collects

  • The site has no accounts, sign-up forms or card payments.
  • Orders, booking requests and messages reach us only when you send them yourself, by email or WhatsApp. We use them to deal with your order or request.
  • Creator Studio uploads stay in your browser during your visit. They are not sent to a server.
  • Hidden Deep saves your game progress in your browser's local storage. It never leaves your device, and you can clear it in your browser settings.

2. Analytics and tracking

We do not use analytics, advertising trackers or tracking cookies on this site.

3. Third-party services

  • Images are loaded from picsum.photos. As with any website that loads files from another server, that service receives your IP address and basic browser details when a page loads.
  • Instagram and WhatsApp open only when you click them. They have their own privacy policies.

4. How long we keep messages

We keep order and booking emails for as long as needed to complete the order and any follow-up. After that, we delete them.

5. Your rights

Under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), you can ask what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email Corestall125@gmail.com and we will respond.

6. Changes to this page

If we change how the site handles information, we will update this page and the date below.
